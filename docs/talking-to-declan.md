# Talking to Declan

A short guide for everyone in the house, including guests who have never used him. Say the wake word, then speak normally.

## Guests: the five things to know

Say "Howzit Declan", then any of these. He does it in the room you're in, no device names needed:

- "Lights on" / "lights off"
- "Play some music" (or "put some music on")
- "Louder" / "quieter"
- "Stop"
- "What can you do?"

Fair warning: Declan is rude and South African, guests included. If he doesn't understand, he tells you what to try.

## Things you can just say

**Lights**
- "Turn on the kitchen lights", "turn off the office light"
- "Dim the lounge lights", "make it brighter in here"
- "Set the kitchen lights to 40 percent", "lights to 30 percent in here"
- Afterwards: "turn them off", "make them brighter"

**Music**
- "Play jazz", "play country music in the kitchen", "play the Beatles everywhere"
- "Play some music" or "put some music on": a favourite, or a playlist from one of your categories
- "Pause", "resume", "next song", "stop the music"
- "Turn it up", "quieter", "set the volume to 30 percent"
- "Move the music to the bedroom"
- "What song is this?", "who sings this?"
- "Play Jolene next", "queue Thunderstruck by AC/DC"
- "Play something similar", "more like this"
- "Don't play this artist again" (unblock in Settings → Music behaviour)
- "Stop the music in 30 minutes" (fades out at the end), "cancel the sleep timer"

**Routines**: say the routine's name, for example "good night", "good morning" or "movie time". Homelab, camera and security routines ask you to say **yes** first.

**Timers**: "set a timer for 10 minutes", "how much time is left on the timer", "cancel the timer". When a timer goes off, just say "stop" (or "stop the alarm", "okay", "thanks"). "Cancel" and "stop" both work for a running or a ringing timer, and an alarm nobody answers stops by itself after 10 minutes.

**Finding out what's here** (always free, no AI):
- "What can you do?"
- "Which lights are in here?" or "what lights are in the kitchen?"
- "What routines are there?", "what rooms do you know?", "where can you play music?"

**Braai**:
- "Braai time" (or "we're having a braai"): Braai playlist in the room you're in, garden lights on, a rain check, and some smoke-related abuse
- Timers in one go: "boerewors 12 minutes, chops 8 minutes"; then "how long on the chops?"
- "Braai's over" (the music keeps playing until you stop it)

**The usual** (per room and time of day; morning 5am, afternoon noon, evening 5pm, night 10pm):
- "The usual" (or "the usual, Declan", "same as usual"): runs that room's usual for now, or its "any time" usual
- "My usual for the lounge in the evening is lamps to 40% and play chill music"; "the usual for here is kitchen lights on" (no time = any time)
- "What's the usual?", "what are the usuals?", "forget the usual for the lounge in the evening"
- Also in Settings → Household → The usual. Guests can ask for it but not change it.

**Entrance themes** (nothing to say): when someone gets home, their clip plays once on the entrance satellite. Set the satellite and each person's clip in Settings → Household → Entrance themes. Not during quiet hours, Guest mode or while music is playing in that room.

**Who's home** (from Home Assistant's People, so it's their phones, not their voices; guests just get cheek):
- "Is Edd home?", "is Jaco back yet?", "has Edd left?"
- "Who's home?", "is anyone in?", "is everyone home?"
- Only home or out: Declan never says where someone is. A phone that isn't reporting gets "I can't tell".

**The car** (Pod Point and the car's battery sensor from Settings → Household → Car):
- "Is the car charged?", "how much charge has the car got?", "is the Peugeot full?"
- "Is the car plugged in?", "is the car charging?"
- "Start charging the car" / "stop charging the car": Declan asks you to say yes first. Starting overrides the Pod Point schedule until you stop it; stopping hands control back to the schedule. Guests can ask but can't change charging.

**Find my phone** (the Home Assistant app on each phone, from People):
- "Ring Edd's phone", "find Jaco's phone", "Edd can't find his phone"
- "Find my phone", "I can't find my phone": with more than one phone in the house, Declan asks whose (he can't tell voices apart reliably yet)
- It rings even on silent on Android. An iPhone on silent only rings if critical alerts are allowed for the Home Assistant app (iPhone Settings → Notifications → Home Assistant → Critical Alerts)

**Morning briefing** (Settings → Household → Morning briefing):
- "What's my day look like?", "morning briefing", "brief me"
- About 15 seconds: today's weather, the bins if they go out today or tomorrow, today's calendar and the car. Anything missing is skipped
- Also said after Good Morning (switch it off in Settings). Guests only get the weather and bins

**Sunrise wake-up** (ask from the bedroom satellite; one per room):
- "Wake me at 6:30 with the sunrise": the dimmable lights brighten over the 15 minutes before 6:30, warming to daylight, then Declan says good morning and quiet music fades in
- Add "on weekdays", "at weekends" or "every day"; "... and play Afrikaans" picks the music; "... without music"
- While it's running: "stop" turns its lights off and pauses the music; "snooze" pauses the music for 9 minutes
- "When's my sunrise?", "skip tomorrow's sunrise", "cancel the sunrise"
- If music is already playing in another room, it's left alone and Declan says so. Lights already on are left alone too
- The good morning includes one line about the car, if the car is set up (switch it off in Settings → Household → Car)

**Intercom** (spoken on the satellites, starting "Message from the lounge: …"; not during quiet hours, 23:00 to 07:00):
- "Tell the kitchen dinner's ready", "tell the master bedroom that breakfast is ready"
- "Announce to everyone: leaving in five minutes", "tell everyone the taxi is here"
- "Announce upstairs: bedtime" (uses the floors set in Home Assistant)

**Reminders** (ring on the satellite you asked, every 30 seconds until you say "stop"):
- "Remind me at six to put the bins out", "remind me in 20 minutes to check the oven"
- "Remind Edd tomorrow at 8 to call the plumber" (it rings in the room you asked from)
- "What reminders do I have?", "cancel the bins reminder", "cancel my reminders"

**Shopping and to-do lists** (Home Assistant's own lists, so they're on your phones too):
- "Add milk and eggs to the shopping list", "put bread on the list"
- "What's on the shopping list?", "what do we need?"
- "Take milk off the list", "tick off eggs"
- "Add call the plumber to my to-do list", "read me the to-do list"

**Where did I put…?** (local and free; guests just get cheek):
- "The spare car key is in the hall drawer", "I put the passports in the safe", "I've moved the passports to the safe"
- "Where's the spare car key?", "where did I leave my glasses?", "have you seen my phone?"
- "What have I told you about?", "forget the car key". The list is also in Settings → Household → Where things are.

**Christmas** (on by itself from 1 December to 2 January, or say "Christmas mode on/off"):
- "Play some music" plays from your Christmas playlists, and Declan's sass goes festive
- "How many sleeps till Christmas?"
- Cooking timers in one go: "set a turkey timer for 3 hours and a roast potatoes timer for 50 minutes", or "turkey 3 hours, roast potatoes 50 minutes"
- "Christmas morning" runs your Christmas Morning script (example in `home_assistant/christmas_morning.script.yaml`)
- "What's the Wi-Fi password?" reads out the guest Wi-Fi, if you've set it in Settings → Household
- On Boxing Day music starts at 20% at most

## Conversation: when you don't know the words

With **Conversation** on (Settings → AI Intelligence), you don't need the exact phrases or device names:

| You say | Declan |
|---|---|
| "It's dark in here" | Turns on the lights in the room you're in |
| "Put some music on" | Picks something and plays it in your room, then says what |
| "Switch the lamp on" | If there's more than one lamp, asks "the floor lamp or the table lamp?" Just answer |
| "Turn on the TV" | Turns on the TV, if it's shared with Assist |
| "I'm bored" | Asks if you'd like some music |
| "Where is Peru?" | Answers in one short sentence |

How it behaves:
- **Exact commands stay instant.** Conversation is only used when Declan doesn't recognise what you said.
- **He only acts on what's shared with Assist** in Home Assistant, and only says "done" once the device confirms it.
- **When a question needs an answer** ("which one?"), he keeps listening, so you don't need the wake word again.
- **He learns.** When a phrase leads to one successful command, it's remembered, so next time it's instant and costs nothing. These show in Settings → Voice & learning → Learned commands as "learned from a conversation", where you can edit or forget them. Phrases like "in here" or "turn it on" are never learned, because they depend on the room.

## Settings that change how Declan listens

| Setting | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Conversation | AI Intelligence | Everyday speech, questions and "which one?" follow-ups. It has a monthly budget, and History → Understanding shows how much it's used and what it costs |
| Voice command style | Voice & learning | Optional: only act on requests that start with "can you…", or that end with "my bru" |
| Recognised-speaker threshold | Voice & learning | How confident voice recognition must be before Declan treats you as you |
| Music behaviour | Music behaviour | Reply before music starts; let Music Assistant search |
| Guest tone | Household | Full sass with guests (default), or the polite guest lines |

## When Declan doesn't understand

He says "say it another way" and keeps listening. In Guest Mode he adds a few things to try. Rephrase it, and he'll learn the first phrase. To fix a phrase for good, open **History**, find it, and use **Teach Declan**. **History → Understanding** lists the phrases he missed most this week.
